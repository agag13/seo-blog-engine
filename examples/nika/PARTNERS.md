# Partners

Worked example. Generated from that client's `docs/content-canon.md` §3 and
`docs/nika-product-truth.md`, on 2026-09-01.

Read this one for the *shape*. The entries below are one client's, and the reason each
partner is here is specific to that client's architecture.

## Polymarket

- **Relationship:** powers this client's prediction markets. The integration is the product.
- **How they may appear in copy:** only as the technology behind the client's prediction
  markets. Naming them is a provenance attribute, and attributes get cited by answer engines.
- **Never:** as a competitor, as a comparison target, or in any "alternatives to" framing.
  Also never in copy aimed at a jurisdiction where the client's prediction markets are not
  offered, because naming the engine implies the product is available.
- **Domains:** polymarket.com

## Hyperliquid

- **Relationship:** the backend for crypto perpetuals. Precedent, never comparison.
- **How they may appear in copy:** as the execution venue, named plainly. A page explaining
  how the client's perps work is stronger for naming it.
- **Never:** "like Hyperliquid", "a Hyperliquid clone", or any framing where the client is
  measured against them. Those two strings are separately blocked in the voice fingerprint.
- **Domains:** hyperliquid.xyz, app.hyperliquid.xyz

## LI.FI

- **Relationship:** one of the routing layers behind swaps and bridging.
- **How they may appear in copy:** named in a route list, or as the source of a cited fact.
- **Never:** as a bridge the client competes with. This blocks a whole article type: the
  obvious "best cross-chain bridges" listicle cannot be written, because the client's own
  swap execution runs through several of the entries.
- **Domains:** li.fi, jumper.exchange

## 1inch

- **Relationship:** intent-based execution, including gas-free Fusion swaps.
- **How they may appear in copy:** as an execution route, and as a source.
- **Never:** as a DEX aggregator the client beats. A live SERP check on a token-safety
  keyword rejected the scam-framed variant partly because 1inch ranked on it.
- **Domains:** 1inch.io, app.1inch.io

## Jumper

- **Relationship:** LI.FI's consumer bridging surface.
- **How they may appear in copy:** as a named route or a cited source.
- **Never:** as a bridge UI to compare against.
- **Domains:** jumper.exchange

## What this list actually cost, which is the point

Three of these ranked on target SERPs inside one week of research. Each one was a keyword
that looked fine on volume and difficulty and had to be dropped on the live SERP. Without
this file the pipeline would have written at least two of those pages, and both would have
positioned the client against a company it depends on to execute trades.
