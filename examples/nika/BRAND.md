# Brand Context

Worked example, abridged. Shows the shape and the level of specificity that actually
changes a draft. A real one is longer.

## Audience
- **Primary**: people who already hold crypto in an exchange account and are deciding
  whether to move to self-custody. They know what a wallet is. They do not know what a
  key export gets them.
- **Secondary**: traders comparing venues on fees and execution, not on branding.
- **Active problems**: "if this exchange freezes, can I get my coins out", "which of these
  bridges is safe", "what does this contract address actually do".
- **Common misconceptions**: that MPC means custodial; that a verified contract means an
  audited contract; that bridges are still the largest category of DeFi loss, which was
  true in 2022 and is not true now.

## Positioning
- **Official entity name**: Nika Finance
- **Homepage**: https://nika.finance
- **Mission**: one app to trade, earn, predict and discover across several asset classes,
  without the user giving up their keys.
- **Distinctive POV**: self-custody is a default, not a feature you upgrade to.
- **What we are NOT**: not the best, not the only honest option, not a place that
  promises outcomes. One honest option among several.
- **Competitors**: named plainly where a comparison is genuinely useful. See PARTNERS.md
  for who is off limits, and it blocks whole article types.

## Editorial Rules

### Always do
- Open answer-first, in about 50 to 60 words a snippet can lift whole.
- Describe the product with plain attributes, not adjectives. Attributes get cited by
  answer engines; adjectives get discarded.
- Cite primary sources with the date they were read, because the claim can expire.
- Carry exactly one honest admission of a limitation per article.
- Pair every diagram with the same information as prose or a table. An SVG's content is
  invisible to answer engines.

### Never do
- No em dashes anywhere. Commas, colons, full stops. Enforced by the voice gate.
- Never claim the brand is best, leading, top, or number one.
- Never write competitively about anyone in PARTNERS.md.
- Never state a product fact that FACTS.md marks CONFLICTED or NOT PUBLISHED.
- Never publish a byline without that author's bio and photo. A named author without them
  measured roughly 8 points worse on authoritativeness than no named author at all.

## Jurisdiction
Some markets are closed to this client entirely, and one whole product line is legally
constrained nearly everywhere. `project.yaml → seo.excluded_markets` carries the list, and
non-US placements lead with self-custody rather than that product line.
