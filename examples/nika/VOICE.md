# Voice Context

Worked example. **This file does not enforce anything.**

That is the point worth understanding before writing one. Prose guidance is read once and
then read past, so everything here that can be measured lives in
`config/voice-fingerprint.yaml` instead, where `voice_check.py` can fail a build on it.
What stays here is the part a number cannot capture.

## Pronoun stance
"You" for the reader. "We" for the brand, sparingly. Never "I" outside a signed byline.

## What the measured gap actually is

Not rule compliance. **Cadence.** Copy can pass every hard rule and still read corporate.

On this client's corpus the founder's own essays ran **42.1% one-sentence paragraphs**,
with **exactly one paragraph over 70 words across 297**. The shipped articles ran 11.7%,
with 62 walls out of 290. Same rules, same facts, completely different rhythm, and the
rhythm is the thing readers feel as "written by a company".

Both numbers are bands in the fingerprint. The gate warns below them.

## Headline patterns
- **Favour**: the question the reader actually typed; a real number when the number is
  load-bearing.
- **Avoid**: "Ultimate Guide", manufactured urgency, and numbers in a headline that the
  body does not immediately support.

## Register
Casual-professional. Contractions are normal, roughly half of contractible pairs. Formal
transitions like "However" and "Furthermore" do not appear in this corpus at all, and the
voice gate blocks them.

## Readability target
Professional audience, but assume no vocabulary they have not already met on an exchange
screen. Explain a term the first time and never again.
